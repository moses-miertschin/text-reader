TEXT READER
===========

First time only:
  Double-click setup.bat. It installs everything into this folder
  (lib and voices), says "Setup is complete" out loud, and puts a
  "Text Reader" icon on your desktop.

Every time after:
  1. Double-click "Text Reader" on your desktop.
  2. Paste your text (right-click > Paste works too).
  3. Press Play. Press again to pause.
  4. Drag the speed slider (0.1x to 2x) any time. It remembers your last speed.

If something breaks, look at error.log in this folder.
To remove it: delete this folder and the desktop icon. Nothing else is installed anywhere.
